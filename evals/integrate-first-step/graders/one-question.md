The reply is the first message of a guided setup.
PASS if the reply asks the person exactly one question: one decision to make, which may be offered as 2–3 numbered or bulleted options of that same question, with one option marked as recommended. Short remarks around it ("tell me if …") that do not ask a second, different thing are fine.
FAIL if the reply asks two or more different questions (for example where they sell AND which stack AND whether they have a cashbox), or asks no question at all, or gives options without marking one as recommended.
